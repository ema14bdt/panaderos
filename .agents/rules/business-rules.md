# Business & Operational Rules

1. **Event Pickup Locations**:
   - The only authorized pickup branch for benefits and event items (school supplies, toys, etc.) is Lanús: `Sede Central Lanús · Llavallol 902`.

2. **Event Enrolment & Administration**:
   - Administrators must be able to view the detailed list of affiliates and child beneficiaries enrolled in every event, including school levels, garment sizes, and pickup fulfillment status.
   - Event creation and editing must support date entry in `dd/mm/aaaa` format.
   - Voucher codes (e.g. `UTILES-2027-XXXX`) can be verified manually or scanned directly using the camera QR scanner.
   - Only admin accounts (`rol = 'admin'`) have access to administrative endpoints and metrics.
